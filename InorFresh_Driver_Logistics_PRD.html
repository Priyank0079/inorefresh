1. Executive Summary

In Plain English

What is this, in one sentence?

We are upgrading InorFresh from a "order now, deliver now" app into a planned daily fish-distribution system — where retailer orders are collected the day before, the warehouse plans delivery routes overnight, and one driver delivers to 10+ shops every morning in a refrigerated vehicle, with full tracking of money, returns, crates, and freshness.

Why are we changing the flow?

The current app is great for instant, single-customer orders. But the fish business actually runs as a wholesale supply chain — many retailers, planned routes, early-morning delivery, returnable crates and ice boxes, and cash collection across many shops. The new flow matches how the business really operates, reduces losses ("leakage"), keeps fish fresh, and gives the warehouse complete control and visibility every single day.

2. The Big Change: Before vs After

Old Flow vs New Flow

⏪ BEFORE (Current App)

On-Demand Delivery
  • Customer orders anytime and wants it immediately
  • System assigns the nearest single rider per order
  • One order = one pickup = one drop
  • No route planning — fully on demand
  • Rider delivers, verifies a 4-digit OTP, done
  • Returns handled later as a separate trip
  • No tracking of crates / ice boxes
  • No cold-chain time tracking
  • No end-of-day reconciliation
  • No ERP — data lives only inside the app

⏩ AFTER (New Logistics Flow)

Planned Distribution
  • Retailer orders the day before (cut-off 8 PM)
  • Warehouse plans full routes overnight
  • One vehicle = 10+ shops in one route
  • Driver accepts the whole route the night before
  • Morning loading + verification (4–6 AM)
  • On-the-spot returns with photo + weight
  • Crates & ice boxes tracked per shop
  • Cold-chain loading→delivery time recorded
  • Driver cannot close day without reconciliation
  • Built on an ERP backbone + daily reports

6. The 12 Phases — Step by Step

Complete Detail
1

Order Collection

Previous Day · by 8 PM
Sales Team
  • Retailers order via Inor Fresh App, WhatsApp, Phone Call, or Sales Executive
  • Sales team verifies product availability, quantity, size & delivery location
  • All orders entered into ERP and marked "Confirmed"
  • Hard cut-off: all orders confirmed before 8:00 PM
Confirmed
2

Route Planning

Previous Day · by 10 PM
Warehouse Planning Team
  • Review all confirmed orders & group retailers by area
  • Calculate total weight & volume, then assign vehicles
  • Vehicles: Own refrigerated 4-wheelers + approved partner vehicles
  • Rules: minimum 10 retailers per vehicle · one route & one driver per vehicle
  • Routes finalized before 10:00 PM
Route Created
3

Driver Assignment

Before Night Closing
Driver
  • Driver receives: Vehicle Number, Route Number, Retailer List, Total Orders, Total Weight, Estimated Route Time
  • Driver must accept the route in the app
Route Accepted
4

Loading Process

4:00 AM – 6:00 AM
Warehouse Team + Driver
  • Warehouse picks, packs, generates invoices, arranges crates & loads the vehicle
  • Driver verifies product quantity, crates, ice boxes & invoice count
  • ERP records loading time, driver name, vehicle number & route number
Vehicle Loaded
5

Delivery Operations

6:00 AM – 10:00 AM
Driver — for each retailer
  • Reach location → click "Arrived" (GPS + arrival time recorded)
  • Deliver products → verify quantity
  • Collect payment (if applicable) → obtain delivery confirmation
Out For Delivery
6

Delivery Confirmation

At Each Shop
Retailer confirms via
  • Option A: OTP Verification
  • Option B: Digital Signature
  • Option C: Photo Proof
  • Auto-updates Retailer App, Logistics App, Warehouse ERP & Accounts System
Delivered
7

Payment Collection

For COD Orders
Driver
  • Collects Cash / UPI / Bank Transfer
  • Enters amount received, payment method & reference number
  • Accounts team receives a real-time update
Payment Recorded
8

Returns & Damage Management

On the Spot
Driver — if retailer reports an issue
  • Records: Wrong Product / Wrong Size / Quality Issue / Damaged / Excess Quantity
  • Uploads photo & returned weight
  • Action: Replacement / Credit Note / Return to Warehouse
Return Logged
9

Returnable Material Collection

At Each Shop
Driver
  • Collects Fish Crates, Ice Boxes, Thermocol Boxes & Plastic Tubs
  • Retailer-wise return quantity recorded
  • System tracks missing materials automatically
Materials Collected
10

Route Completion

After Final Delivery
Driver
  • Clicks "Complete Route"
  • System records route completion time, total stops, total distance & total deliveries
Route Completed
11

Warehouse Reconciliation

On Vehicle Return
Warehouse Team
  • Verifies returned fish, empty crates, ice boxes, packaging materials & cash collections
  • All data reconciled in ERP
  • Driver cannot close the day without completing reconciliation
Reconciled
12

Daily Logistics Report

Auto-Generated
System
  • Sales: Total Orders, Revenue, Weight Delivered
  • Logistics: Vehicles Used, Routes Completed, On-Time %
  • Returns: Returned KG, Damaged KG
  • Materials: Crates Returned, Crates Missing
  • Collections: Cash, UPI, Credit Sales
Day Closed

8. Business Rules

Non-Negotiables
  • All retailer orders must be confirmed before 8:00 PM
  • Route planning completed before 10:00 PM
  • Minimum 10 orders per vehicle
  • Delivery window: 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM
  • Every delivery requires confirmation
  • Every return requires photo evidence
  • All collections must be reconciled before closing the day
  • Driver cannot close route without completing reconciliation

10. Strategic Benefits

Why This Matters for the Business

💧 Reduces Delivery Leakage

Every order, payment, return & crate is tracked — far less loss and theft.

❄️ Cold-Chain Compliance

Time tracking from loading to delivery keeps seafood fresh & safe.

📦 Asset Protection

Crates, ice boxes & tubs are tracked per shop — missing items flagged instantly.

💰 Better Cash Collection

Real-time payment capture with proof means accounts always match reality.

🗺️ Full Visibility

Warehouse sees every vehicle, delay & route deviation live, all day.

📊 End-to-End Supply Chain

One connected system from retailer order to daily report — no blind spots.

📌 Summary for the Client

Today's app is built for instant single-customer delivery. The new flow turns InorFresh into a complete daily fish-distribution operation: retailers order the day before, the warehouse plans smart routes overnight, one driver serves 10+ shops every morning in a cold vehicle, and at the end of the day every rupee, every return, and every crate is reconciled — with an automatic daily report. The result is less loss, fresher fish, accurate cash, and complete control over the entire supply chain.
